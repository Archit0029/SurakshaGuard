The Crisis

Over ₹1,750+ Crores Lost to Payment Cyberfraud in India

UPI processes over 14 Billion transactions monthly. Scammers don't hack bank servers; they exploit human fear, urgency, and confusion.

⚡

Electricity & Discom Panic Extortion

"Your power will be cut tonight at 9:30 PM. Pay ₹10 immediately to our DISCOM officer via Google Pay." Victims panic and pay thousands under duress.

🎁

Reverse-Payment QR Traps

"Scan this QR code and enter your PIN to claim your ₹2,500 lottery/cashback reward." Victims enter their PIN thinking they are receiving money.

🖥️

Remote Screen-Sharing Takeover

Victims are coerced over a phone call to install AnyDesk or TeamViewer QuickSupport, allowing scammers to view bank OTPs and balances in real time.

💬

Unauthorized Bank Debit OTP Extortion

Scammers initiate bank debit transactions while keeping the victim engaged on call, tricking them into revealing the OTP or approving the deduction.
